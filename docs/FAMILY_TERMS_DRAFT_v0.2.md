## Draft family wording

> **Starting.** Your child can have a free 30-minute taster session first, which includes an initial conversation about what they need. There is no fixed contract. You commit to an initial three months of lessons, and after that you can stop at any time by giving one month's notice.
>
> **Paying.** We send you a statement at the end of each month for the lessons your child has already had, and for any lessons that are chargeable under the cancellation rules below. You pay by bank transfer, using the details and the reference on the statement. Each enrolled sibling in an eligible family receives 10% off monthly lesson charges. There are no late fees. If a payment is more than a month overdue, we will contact you first, and lessons may be paused until it is sorted out.
>
> **Cancelling a lesson.** Please give at least 24 hours' notice. A lesson cancelled with less notice is charged, except that each child's first late cancellation is free. Each child's first no-show is also free, and a later one is charged. A tutor waits 15 minutes from the start of the lesson before recording that a child did not attend. If your tutor cancels, we arrange a replacement time with you and you are not charged. If you are unhappy with the tuition, we can discuss arranging lessons with another tutor. Your statutory rights continue to apply.
>
> **Your child's account.** Your child will have a separate learner account, and a parent or guardian may quietly observe from the room.

**Notes for the reviewer** (not part of the wording):

- **Late cancellation, repeat charge.** The answers say later late cancellations are "charged" but not how much. The software charges the lesson's own price (the same as a repeat no-show), and the owners can set a fixed fee instead. This is an assumption to confirm.
- **Overdue.** The answers say lessons are paused once a payment is more than one month overdue. The software does not do this by itself: a person pauses them, with a reason, after the family has been contacted. Whether the wording above should promise contact first is for the owners and the solicitor.
- **Three months, then a month's notice.** Not yet defined: what "initial three months" is counted from, whether the three months are paid in full if the family leaves earlier, and what happens to a lesson booked in the notice month.
- **Sibling discount.** "Each enrolled sibling in an eligible family": whether a family needs two children before any child gets the 10% is still open (question 3 in section 5 of the 7 Nov answers file).
- **Prepaid lessons** and the six-month expiry from v0.2 are **removed**: the 14 Nov answers say families are invoiced monthly after the lessons, so prepaid blocks are not sold.
- **Recording.** v0.2 said "LearnThrive plans to record lessons". That sentence is **not** in v0.3 and must not appear anywhere public until the recording impact assessment is complete. The 14 Nov answers say tutors and students will both be recorded once the system is active, kept for 30 days unless needed for safeguarding or legal reasons. The enrolment wording must say so before any child is recorded.
- **Card payments.** The 14 Nov answers say parents will pay by card on the website. The first version launches without that, so the paragraph above says bank transfer. When card payment is added, this paragraph changes.
- Not drafted yet: the 14-day distance-contract cancellation notice and the request to start tuition inside it (C5), and the complaints route (C6).

## Draft parent sign-in guide

> Open the LearnThrive portal and sign in with the username and temporary password sent to you. Change the password when prompted. Use your parent account to see your child's upcoming lessons. At the scheduled time, use the join button for the relevant lesson. Your child will have a separate username. Your **Fees** page shows each month's statement and how to pay.
>
> If you forget your password, select the password reset option. The reset link goes to the email connected to the account. For help signing in or finding a lesson, email info@learnthrivetuition.co.uk.
