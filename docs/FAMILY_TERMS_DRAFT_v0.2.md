## Draft family wording

> We email your parent or guardian a bill at the end of each month for tuition already delivered and any applicable missed-lesson charges. They can pay for a lesson once it has taken place. We keep your agreed lesson rate fixed for the annual contract. Each enrolled sibling in an eligible family receives 10% off monthly lesson charges. New learners can have a free 30-minute taster.
>
> Give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel without a charge. Each child's first late cancellation is free. Under the draft rules, each child's first no-show is also free, and repeat no-shows are charged at the full lesson price. If your tutor cancels, we arrange a replacement time with you. If you are unhappy with tuition, we can discuss arranging lessons with another tutor.
>
> Prepaid lessons must be used within six months of purchase under the draft terms. Unused lessons expire without a discretionary refund. Your statutory rights continue to apply. Your child will have a separate learner account and a parent or guardian may quietly observe from the room. LearnThrive plans to record lessons for safeguarding and will explain the arrangements before enrolment.

**Notes for the reviewer** (not part of the wording):

- "Each enrolled sibling in an eligible family": the answers say the discount applies to every enrolled sibling "including the first enrolled child". Whether a family needs two children before it applies is open (question 3 in section 5 of the answers file).
- "Under the draft rules ... first no-show is also free": a working interpretation, tracked separately from the first late cancellation for each child.
- "Prepaid lessons": the answers also say billing is monthly in arrears. Whether prepaid lessons are sold at all is open.
- "LearnThrive plans to record lessons": the current public pages say lessons are **not** recorded by default. This sentence must not appear anywhere public until the recording impact assessment is complete.
- Not drafted yet: the 14-day distance-contract cancellation notice and the request to start tuition inside it (C5), the annual contract and termination terms, and the complaints route (C6).

## Draft parent sign-in guide

> Open the LearnThrive portal and sign in with the username and temporary password sent to you. Change the password when prompted. Use your parent account to see your child's upcoming lessons. At the scheduled time, use the join button for the relevant lesson. Your child will have a separate username.
>
> If you forget your password, select the password reset option. The reset link goes to the email connected to the account. For help signing in or finding a lesson, email info@learnthrivetuition.co.uk.
